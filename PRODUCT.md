# Product

**What:** A single submission page for an M.Des assignment ("Design with AI", HIT, assignment 2: The Latent Space). Author: Tzahi Shemesh.

**Work:** "Corpus: The Client Said", a response to Trevor Paglen's *Adversarially Evolved Hallucinations* (2017). Four words clients use with interior designers (חמים, נקי, יוקרתי, כמו במלון), each generated 30 times by Flux Dev, averaged into a pixel-mean "hallucination". Two layers per word: the bare prompt (the machine) and an Israeli apartment described by the designer.

**Audience:** Course lecturers (Eitan, Daphne) and classmates, reading on laptops and phones.

**Required content:** original work name, artist and image; response (100-200 words) with quote; the creation, the move and tools; 2-3 sentences on how it answers rather than imitates.

**Mode:** Read / Experience. A quiet exhibition catalog that scrolls.

**Brief from the author:** white background, clean and professional, readable and consistent typography, correct alignment, a scrolling page. Hebrew, fully RTL; English stays LTR.
